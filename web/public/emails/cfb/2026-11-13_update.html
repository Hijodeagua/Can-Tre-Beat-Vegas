The Elo-only columns (win prob, expected score, total) come from the pure Elo rating alone (home edge applied except at neutral sites, marked N) and have never seen the efficiency features or a market number. On this slate the two disagree on 6 games, marked ≠ Elo: there the pick is the model's call and the Elo columns show what rating alone would say. The Elo-only expected score is the rating's implied margin carved out of a matchup-specific total (each program's recent points for/against, shrunk to the FBS mean); read it as an average, not a literal final.
