Two different numbers. The pick and the pick model win prob come from the elo + efficiency (random forest) model, which starts from the Elo rating and adds each team's efficiency features; both are quoted for the picked side. The Elo-only columns (win prob, line, expected score, total) come from the pure Elo rating alone (home edge applied except at neutral sites, marked N; a side off its bye gets the rest edge) and have never seen the efficiency features or a market number. On this slate the two agree on every pick. The Elo-only line is the rating's own spread from the home side; the expected score is that margin carved out of a matchup-specific total (each team's recent points for/against, shrunk to the league mean) — read it as an average, not a literal final.
