Key features

Source: docs/MODEL_FEATURES.md · as of 2026-11-02

Model

TypeGlued club Elo (one pool per country, UEFA cross-play) + random-forest outcome model over Elo, squad economics, rolling xG / shots form and the Understat advanced layer
TargetThree-way match outcome (home win / draw / away win); pick = the likeliest class
Training windowReplay history across all pools (2010-11 onward by league); outcome model refit in-run daily
Leaguesepl, championship, bundesliga, bundesliga_2, la_liga, la_liga_2, serie_a, serie_b, ligue_1, ligue_2, mls

Inputs (from docs/MODEL_FEATURES.md)

  • Home Elo, Away Elo — each club's Elo as its own column, so a rating level can matter, not only the gap (a big favourite above 1625 Elo wins 77% of the time; the same gap below 1325 wins 56%)
  • elogap — the venue-adjusted difference, alongside the two above
  • spenddiffz, netdiffz, valuediffz, wagediffz — squad economics differentials, z-scored within league-season (wages still unpopulated)
  • xgnetdiff — rolling xG net over 10 league matches, home − away (Understat; live again since 2026-09-17)
  • sotnetdiff — rolling shots-on-target net (football-data.co.uk)
  • Advanced Understat layer (soccer/clubs/model/advanced.py): xgfor/againstewmdiff, npxgfor/againstewmdiff (5-match half-life) and the rolling-10 versions; homeattvsawaydef, awayattvshomedef and their npxG versions (home-only attack vs away-only defence); xgpershotdiff; deepdiff, deepsharediff (field-tilt proxy — Understat publishes no possession), ppdadiff, xptsewmdiff; restdiff, congestion14home/away, uefa7home/away from the whole calendar
  • Context — one-hot league, tier, season index: one pooled model, not per-league sub-models (sub-models scored worse in 9 of 11 leagues)
